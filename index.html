About Me

I'm a mechanical engineering student who likes building things that have to actually work — under load, under pressure, or in zero gravity.

I'm certified in Autodesk Inventor and work regularly in Solidworks and MATLAB, with hands-on shop experience in 3D printing (Bambu) and laser cutting/engraving. Outside the lab, I've spent several years teaching math, engineering fundamentals, and cello. Feel free to scroll down to learn more about my projects and my hobbies!

  • Aerospace payload & systems design
  • Mechanical design & CAD prototyping
  • Underwater robotics & fluid systems
  • Teaching & making technical ideas clear

SHEET 01/04NASA HUNCH

Micro-gravity Nanolab

2U payload — fluid dynamics research

Led the team engineering a 2U payload studying zero-gravity fluid dynamics for agricultural applications. Owned the mechanical design of the housing and fluid-handling assembly, iterating through several CAD revisions in Inventor to fit sensors, pumps, and structural supports inside the strict 2U envelope, then validated sealing and fluid behavior through ground testing.

SHEET 02/04NASA TECHRISE

High-Altitude Microplastics Collector

National student challenge

Designed a high-altitude balloon experiment to collect atmospheric microplastics, selected as 1 of 60 teams nationwide out of 720+ applicants. Developed the sampling mechanism and enclosure, working through the weight, power, and thermal constraints typical of near-space payloads, and coordinated on data logging and recovery procedures.

SHEET 03/04SEAPERCH

Underwater ROV

U.S. Navy SeaPerch — NY/NJ Regional

Built and piloted a remotely operated underwater vehicle for the regional SeaPerch competition. Designed the frame and buoyancy layout to balance stability with maneuverability, handled the control-side wiring and waterproofing for the tether and thrusters, then piloted the finished ROV through the competition's timed obstacle and task course.

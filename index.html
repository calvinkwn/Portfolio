About

I'm a mechanical engineering student who likes building things that have to actually work — under load, under pressure, or in zero gravity. My background spans aerospace payload design, underwater robotics, and rapid prototyping, and I'm drawn to projects where the constraints are tight and the margin for error is small.

I'm certified in Autodesk Inventor and work regularly in Onshape and MATLAB, with hands-on shop experience in 3D printing (Bambu) and laser cutting/engraving. Outside the lab, I've spent several years teaching — math, engineering fundamentals, and cello — which shapes how I like to explain technical work: clearly, and without unnecessary jargon.
